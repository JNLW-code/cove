Where this differs from your screenshots, and why

  • No lyrics and the music tile is Spotify, as you asked. The island is 162 pt tall with tiles only, taller when Claude rows or a card are showing.
  • Toolbar keeps settings, edit, volume, theme and recent apps. Brightness, AirPlay, folder, tray and the apps grid are out (no public API, or Later).
  • Cards get a thin edge in the status colour (orange, green, accent). The screenshots have none, but your colour rules win over the screenshots.
  • Light theme Allow is a dark pill with white text, because a white pill vanishes on a white island. Dark theme keeps the white pill from ref 2.
  • Blue tint of the screenshots is gone: system greys are neutral.
  • Claude rows sit in one full-width strip above the tiles; Needs-you and Done cards take over the island, as you chose.
